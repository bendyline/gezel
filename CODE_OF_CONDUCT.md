# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Be nice. Do not be a jerk.

Treat other people with respect, patience, and good faith. Disagreement is
welcome when it is constructive and focused on the work. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, personal attacks, trolling, deliberate disruption,
and the publication of another person's private information are not welcome.

## Scope

This standard applies in this repository and in other public or private spaces
when someone is representing the Gezel project or its community.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to
[support@bendyline.com](mailto:support@bendyline.com). Project maintainers may
remove or edit content and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when they reasonably believe this Code of Conduct has been
violated.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and fairly as circumstances allow. The
privacy and safety of reporters will be respected to the extent reasonably
possible.

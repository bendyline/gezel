Additional Notes

1. What Gezel does

Gezel is a free, open-source, local-first app for working with AI. All gezel definitions, chat sessions with local AI, project files, and memories are stored on your computer.

When using the application, Bendyline LLC does not collect, store, or transmit your data. Gezel does facilitate the use of third-party software and connections at your direction, and these are subject to their respective terms.

Because Gezel is local-first by design, what you do with your gezels is your own - we have no view into it, and that's intentional.

Gezel is still in very early preview. Consider using it on a device separate from your valued work or personal data.

A note from us: nobody knows yet what the long-term personal and societal consequences of deep AI use - or of building para-social relationships with AI - will be. AI can be genuinely useful, and we hope Gezel helps you do great things with it. But AI is also very good at shaping how you feel - please use it thoughtfully, and look after your own wellbeing.

2. Third-party model providers

Gezel routes chat messages to the language-model provider you configure (GitHub Copilot, OpenAI, or others). When you use one, your messages are sent to that provider over the public internet, subject to their terms and privacy policy. Bendyline LLC is not a party to that relationship.

While Gezel can work with cloud AI providers like OpenAI or Anthropic, it may use cloud AI in an inefficient or unbounded manner. If you connect Gezel to your cloud accounts, please make sure you have appropriate budget limits turned on.

4. Third-party components Gezel downloads

On first boot, Gezel downloads the Playwright MCP server (Apache-2.0) from the public npm registry into your own Gezel home folder. Playwright also downloads a Chromium build of about 281 MB from its own servers, under Chromium's BSD-3-Clause license.

GitHub Copilot support is optional and is not downloaded on first boot. If you choose it and do not already have a compatible Copilot CLI, Gezel can install the GitHub Copilot SDK and CLI into your Gezel home on demand. The CLI is proprietary, under the free-of-charge GitHub Copilot CLI License. Gezel can instead use a compatible Copilot CLI you already installed or explicitly configured.

This downloaded software is not part of Gezel and is not redistributed by Bendyline LLC. Each component is governed by its own license as published by its author, not by the MIT License above, and it is not covered by the bundled inventory described in section 3.

Model weights work the same way: anything you download through Gezel comes from its upstream source under its own license. See the "Catalog models" section of NOTICE.md before relying on any model commercially.

5. Auto-update

Gezel checks for updates on launch by querying public GitHub release metadata. No personal data is sent in this check. You can disable auto-update by editing your settings.

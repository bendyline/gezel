Qwen 3.8 27B Q4 and Gemma 4 12B Q4 completed **516 planned trials** on an
Apple M5 Max with 128 GB of unified memory, using MLX and llama.cpp with
native 0.1.54. Core, productivity, developer and complex-work each have three
repetitions per model, engine and scenario: 172 treatments in total. The
campaign began October 27 local time (October 28 UTC) and finished November 2.

Qwen on llama.cpp passed **104/129** eligible trials, versus **92/128** on
MLX. Gemma passed **86/128** on llama.cpp and **67/129** on MLX. The two
original infrastructure exclusions remain excluded from capability scores;
their results and performance remain in the download. No trial was replaced
or reclassified for publication. The frozen fixed-score means were 8.26,
7.88, 7.85 and 7.19 respectively. Fixed scores and advisory artifact quality
measure different things and remain separate.

There are **215 numeric artifact reviews**, including 80 artifacts from
failed trials. Twelve A/B readouts had incompatible judge specifications;
one declared code-review artifact was missing. Neither group receives an
invented numeric score. The other 288 trials had no applicable rubric.
The downloads retain exact prompts, axes, scores and justifications. These
were source reviews capped at 20 KiB, not full visual or interactive reviews;
two pet-shop artifacts were truncated at that cap.

Gemma's median in-trial decode rate was 71.76 tokens/s on llama.cpp and 36
on MLX. Qwen's was 28.45 and 28 respectively. These engine estimates differ:
MLX uses the median of reported pulses; llama.cpp divides generated tokens
by decode time. MLX granted context is unknown, and macOS GPU
utilization is unavailable. Performance includes failed and excluded trials,
with sample counts, latency, token usage, process RSS and system memory.

MLX prefill rates were recovered on November 3 from **2,175 first-token
diagnostics across all 258 original MLX trials**. The medians of trial
medians are **547 tok/s for Qwen and 1,084 tok/s for Gemma**. These rounded,
partial provider-turn observations are marked **observed** in the tables;
they are not duration-weighted aggregate request throughput. Exact MLX
prefill times remain unknown. The [recovery data](../../assets/scorecards/2026-10-27-m5max-native-0-1-54/mlx-prefill-recovery.json)
retains every observation and source hashes. The original trial archive,
scores, exclusions and judgments were not changed.

Quantization and speculative decoding also differ. Qwen uses affine 4-bit
MLX weights versus UD-Q4_K_M GGUF, with MTP and possible MLX grammar fallback.
Gemma uses MXFP4 on MLX without observed MTP, versus qat UD-Q4_K_XL with an
external Q4_0 MTP drafter on llama.cpp. The report records these conditions
and diagnoses evaluator defects, timeouts, stalled chats and incomplete work.

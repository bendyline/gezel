# The Video Generator

The **Video Generator** is the moving-picture sibling of the Image
Generator: describe a shot, get a short clip. Product teasers, animated
backgrounds, little social moments — generated on whatever video model
your device or provider offers, delivered into the project's artifacts.

Like its sibling, it's fixed-function by design: no file editing, no
workspace access, just generation. Short clips take real compute — on
local hardware expect renders to be measured in minutes, not seconds,
and lean on the smallest length that sells the idea.

# Roles at a glance

A gezel's **role** is its trade: the bundle of skills, tools, and habits it brings to your crew. Roles aren't just labels — each one comes with a default character, a curated toolkit, and a model floor (the minimum model tier the role needs to work unsupervised).

::handboek-role-summary-table

Each role has its own article in this section with the full picture: the character it starts with, every tool in its kit, and the craftbooks it runs well. And roles are starting points, not cages — you can rename, re-equip, and rewrite any gezel after it's created.

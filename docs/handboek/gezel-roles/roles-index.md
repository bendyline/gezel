# Roles at a glance

A **role** is a crew member's trade: the bundle of skills, tools, and habits they bring to the work. Roles aren't just labels — each one comes with a default character, a curated toolkit, and a model floor (the minimum model tier the role needs to work unsupervised).

::handboek-role-summary-table

Each role has its own article in this section with the full picture: the character it starts with, every tool in its kit, and the plans it runs well. And roles are starting points, not cages — you can rename, re-equip, and rewrite any crew member after they're created.

To browse the ready-made characters outside the app, see [every role template](https://gezel.com/docs/role-catalog/) on gezel.com.

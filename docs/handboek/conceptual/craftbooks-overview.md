# Plans

A **plan** is a written-down procedure Gezel can follow: a sequence of steps, each naming the role that runs it and what "done" means. Think of it as a recipe card for knowledge work — research this, draft that, check it, deliver the result. The file a plan is written in is called a *craftbook*, which is the word the command line and the technical articles use.

## Why procedures beat prompts

Asking for "a market report" in a chat leaves everything to improvisation. Running the research-report plan instead gives you a pipeline: scope the question, gather sources, draft, and gate the result against real checks before it reaches you. Steps can't advance until their checks pass, so quality is enforced rather than hoped for. With most models one crew member walks every step; with small models on your own computer, each step goes to a specialist for that role. See [Generalist mode](generalist-mode.md).

## Using plans

Most plans can be started straight from chat — each one declares trigger phrases, and Gezel offers the plan when a request matches one. You can also browse and start them from the Plans area, or ask the Meester what's available for the job at hand.

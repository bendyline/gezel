# What is gezel?

Gezel gives you a **crew of AI companions** — *gezellen*, plural of *gezel*,
the Dutch word for a craftsman's companion — and puts them to work on your
projects. Instead
of one anonymous chat box, you assemble a small workshop: a researcher who
digs into questions, a copywriter who drafts, a reviewer who checks, a
developer who builds. Each gezel has a name, a face, a role, and a memory.

## A crew, not a chat

Most AI tools hand you a blank text field. Gezel hands you people — or the
warm, carved-wood version of them. You meet your **Meester** first: the
guildmaster who helps you figure out which companions you need and sets them
up for you. From there, work happens the way it does in a real workshop:
you talk to the right person for the job, and they remember what you've done
together.

## Your data stays yours {[pullQuote]}

Everything gezel creates lives on your own disk as ordinary files you can
open, copy, and back up. There is no gezel cloud account, no server of ours
between you and your work. Gezel talks directly to whichever AI provider you
choose — including models that run entirely on your own machine.

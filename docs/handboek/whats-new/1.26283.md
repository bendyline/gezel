# 1.26283 — 10 October 2026

Four days after **1.26279**. Until now, putting your **gezels** to work on your own files meant setting up a project and deciding what to ask. Gezels are the named AI craftspeople on your crew. Now you can hand them a folder, such as your Pictures, your Documents or a codebase. They read it, never change it, and work on it overnight. In the morning you find a short report, albums of your recent photos, and fixes for you to approve.

If you read only one thing: choose **File → Add Folder…** or answer the question on Home, and see what your crew makes of your Pictures folder by tomorrow morning.

## Hand your crew a folder

There are three ways in: **Add Folder…** in the File menu (Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+O), the **Existing Folder** choice under **New Project**, and a new question on Home: "Which folders should your crew look after?" Pictures and Documents are ticked there, and any code folders gezel finds are listed. Home keeps asking until you add a folder or skip the question.

Before anything is added, you see what the folder holds, such as "12,480 photos · 1,204 only in the cloud", and what your crew will do with it tonight.

- **Read-only, always.** Gezel reads the folder and builds a search index of it. It never changes, moves or deletes a file unless you ask and approve. That includes git's own housekeeping files in a code folder. Everything gezel learns about the folder, such as its index, photo descriptions and summaries, lives in gezel's own folder, not yours. Earlier versions kept a project's search index in a `.gezel` folder inside your folder. It is moved out the first time gezel starts, not rebuilt, so nothing is read again.
- **Files that live only in the cloud stay there.** With iCloud's Optimize Storage or OneDrive's Files On-Demand, files that are not on this computer are listed by name and date, and never downloaded. A Photos library belongs to the Photos app, so gezel leaves it alone.
- **A crew comes with it.** Every folder gets a **Boekwachter**, the crew member who reads a folder, describes it and files issues about it. A code folder also gets a **Builder**, which lets your crew draft fixes overnight. A photo folder gets a **Curator**, a new role that looks after your photos ([The Curator](../gezel-roles/role-curator.md)). They are added once, when you add the folder. If you take one off, they stay off.
- **The folder's Overview gives you a first look.** For example: "12,480 photos from 2009 to 2026 · about 3,100 look like duplicates · 840 screenshots", and how long the first pass will take, when that is an hour or more. A file you open in the folder shows an **About this file** line with the Boekwachter's one-line summary.
- **Changes arrive as proposals.** When your crew wants to change something, say a fix for a problem in your code, you get a **change proposal** to read and apply yourself. Proposals can now also move and copy files and create folders. Each of these is its own row, applied after the edits and never over an existing file. A problem the Boekwachter found is marked fixed only when you apply its fix, and opens again if you dismiss the proposal.

New folders are worked on overnight by default, during the **Night Shift**, the work your crew does while you sleep. A code folder gets reviews of its structure, dependencies, security and tests, plus fixes drafted for what they find. Fixes are now drafted only for code folders. A photo folder gets albums. Overnight work is switched on only for gezels that run on a model on this computer. Nothing that would send your files to a cloud model is switched on for you. To stop overnight work on a folder, turn off **Work on this folder overnight** on its Overview. The folder stays searchable. See [Adding folders](../conceptual/adding-folders.md).

## Mornings: what your crew did overnight

When your crew worked overnight, Home opens on **This morning**. It leads with what needs a decision: change proposals, then new reports. Then it shows what finished, new albums and **On this day** for your photo folders, and what is already queued for tonight. It stays until you press **Dismiss**.

- **A quiet night says why.** When there was work waiting and none got done, the morning card gives the reason: the computer was asleep, gezel was not running, you stopped the shift, it ran on battery, or your cloud quota was being saved. Where there is a fix, the card offers it: **Add a folder**, **Night Shift settings** or **Keep gezel running at night**.
- **Each folder leaves a short report, made without a model**, so it costs nothing. Photos: recent outings, photos from this day in earlier years, and exact duplicates. Documents: what was added or changed. Code: the files that change often and carry problems, the files everything else depends on, and open issues by severity. On the first morning of a new week, **Your crew's week** recaps every night.
- **Queue something for tonight.** A one-time craftbook (a written recipe of steps a crew follows) has a **Tonight** choice, in the chat's task bar and in the New Task dialog, so it runs during the Night Shift instead of now.
- **New in Settings → General → Night Shift:** **Start Gezel when I log in, without opening a window** keeps gezel in the tray, or the menu bar on a Mac, from the moment you log in. It is available in the installed app only. **Pause night work while this computer runs on battery** is on by default: unplug and the shift stops, plug in and it continues. Answering **Yes** to "Let your crew work while you sleep?" on Home turns both on, along with the Night Shift itself and keeping the computer awake while it runs.
- **One Mac setting is gone.** **Wake this machine when the window opens** never worked, because macOS lets only administrators schedule a wake. Settings now says so. Leave the Mac plugged in and awake for the night.

## Photos, video and sound, found by what is in them

Finding a photo used to mean remembering its name or folder. Now the search box finds photos by what they show, such as "dog on the beach" or "birthday cake". It also finds the moment in a video or recording where something happens. Results say **Photo**, **Video · 1:20–1:50** or **Sound**, photos show a thumbnail, and opening a video or recording plays it from the moment that matched. Your gezels can search your photos the same way.

This replaces the earlier image search with one model, Google's EmbeddingGemma 2, that understands photos, video and sound together. It reads photos only while your computer is idle or during the Night Shift, never during a chat. Photos you had already indexed are read again with the new model the same way.

- **It is on by default, and it downloads about 540 MB** the first time gezel starts, if your security settings allow gezel to use the network. If they don't, photos are found by name and description as before.
- **Video and sound need two more things.** Sound support is about 340 MB more. It downloads when gezel first indexes a video or recording, or when you press **Download sound support now**. You also need **ffmpeg**, which gezel does not include. Install it from ffmpeg.org, or with Homebrew on a Mac, then press **Check for ffmpeg again**. Photos work without either.
- **Settings → Image recognition → Search photos, video and sound** turns it off, and **Photo detail** chooses how closely each photo is read: **Quick**, **Balanced**, **Detailed** (the default) or **Finest**. At **Detailed**, a recent Mac reads about one photo every two seconds. Changing the level reads every photo again in the background.
- **More kinds of photo.** On a Mac, HEIC photos from iPhones are read. On every computer, camera RAW files (DNG, CR2, NEF, ARW, RAF, ORF, RW2) are read too. For each photo, gezel records the date and the camera and lens it was taken with.
- **Albums.** Overnight, the Curator turns each recent outing into an album: a slideshow of the frames that tell the day, in order and captioned, with gentle motion between them. Open one from the folder's Overview or **This morning** to play it, edit it, or export it as a video. An album keeps its own smaller copies of the photos, with where they were taken removed. **Copy to a folder…** gathers the full-size originals in one place and never replaces a file.
- **Where a photo was taken stays private.** Gezels using a cloud model are never told, and reports and albums never mention it.

## Gezels remember you

Telling one gezel your name, your job or that you prefer metric used to stay with that gezel. Now there is one shared set of notes about you, read by every gezel. It is in **Settings → About You**, listed by day. Correct or remove anything there, and gezels read the new version from their next message. It starts empty: notes your gezels made before this release stay in their own memories.

- **A gezel's Memories tab can be edited**, both its daily notes and its lessons. Lines you put under a `## Pinned` heading in lessons are kept word for word when the gezel rewrites its lessons.
- **Gezels remember corrections.** A mistake and its fix are saved as a correction. When a message repeats a mistake you have made before, the gezel sees that note first. The Language Trainer below uses this to keep working on what you get wrong.
- **Notes about you show up in search**, labelled "Memory · About you".

See [Memory, documents, and the shared library](../conceptual/memory-and-documents.md).

## Social mode: character and growth

**Social mode** is a new switch in **Settings → General**. With it on, your gezels talk in their own character, you can watch them grow, and opening a chat shows what they have waiting for you. With it off, they work in a plain, even voice. It is off by default on the desktop.

**If you use the desktop, this changes what you see.** The **Growth** tab, level badges, "I just reached level 3!" in chat and level-up notifications used to be always on. Now they show only with social mode on. Your gezels keep earning experience either way, so turning it on brings everything back where it was.

- **Every gezel has a character:** a temperament (warm, calm, lively, precise, wry or patient), one of twelve quirks, a style (plain, playful or formal) and how much small talk they make, from **Quiet** to **Talkative**. Existing gezels have been given one. With social mode on, change it on the gezel's **Appearance** tab, which shows the exact lines the gezel reads about itself. Character decides how a gezel talks, not what it does, and never makes it skip a step.
- **Growth counts more of what a gezel learns:** corrections it takes on board and worked examples now earn experience. Chatting more still earns nothing.
- **"What's waiting."** Opening a gezel's chat, or a Just Chat project, shows at most one thing: a question it asked that you haven't answered, or work it finished in the last three days, with **Open**. If nothing is waiting, there is no card. Dismiss one and it stays gone.

See [Social mode](../conceptual/character-and-growth.md).

## New games and lessons

Some kinds of project come with their own crew, tools and an **Output** tab you can play or practise in. Several got bigger:

- **Word Clues** is a new word game. Sixteen words are on the board: six are yours, nine are bystanders, and one is the trap. Two new gezels take turns giving clues, the lively **Woordsmid** and the careful **Puzzelaar**.
- **Language Trainer** has role-play scenes on its Output tab, such as a café, a market, a pharmacy, lost luggage and a doctor's visit, over three levels. The tutor's corrections appear under each reply and come back as review cards after a day, three days and a week. The tab shows your level, your day streak and the reviews that are due.
- **Flashcards** can **Make cards from notes**: paste your notes and get four to eight cards.
- **Fitness Coach** has **Log a session** on its Output tab and a **Weekly recap**: the numbers come from your log, and the coach adds one suggestion. It asks before scheduling a Sunday recap.
- **Checkers, Chess and Go** have a new table-talk style, **In-character**, now the default, which follows your gezel's character. Moves are more reliable on small models.
- Output pages follow your light or dark theme.

These apply to projects you start from now on. Projects you already have keep the version they were created with.

## Notifications only when something happened

Gezel now notifies you only when something actually happened: a gezel asked you a question, work you asked for is finished, your crew worked overnight, a gezel reached a new level (with social mode on), or a project worked out that something is due. Flashcards and the Language Trainer, for example, remind you when reviews come up. Gezel never notifies you just because time passed.

- **Three a day at most, by default.** Choose **Off**, **1**, **3**, **5** or **10** in **Settings → General → Notifications**. Things that happen together arrive as one notification, such as "3 things are waiting". Anything over the limit is still waiting in **Activity** and in its chat.
- **Nothing while you are looking.** While a gezel window has focus, you get no notifications.
- **They arrive with the window closed.** The app now sends them itself, so they reach you as long as gezel is running, including from the tray.

See [Notifications](../conceptual/notifications.md).

## Security

None of these changes anything you do.

- **A gezel using git can no longer write files, read files outside the project, or run programs your git settings name.** Gezels get a short list of git commands, each with its own allowed options.
- **A browser script a gezel wants to run needs your approval of that exact script.** Changing the script, or anything it loads, asks again. The script never gets gezel's own credentials, and it is unavailable while External Services is off.
- **A project's own settings can no longer change how packages are installed.** Installs ignore install-time plugins and hook files a project carries, never switch to a different installer version the project asks for, and always download from the public registry, whatever a planted `.npmrc` says.
- **Where your photos were taken is never sent to a cloud model.**

## Smaller things

- **Take a photo from the chat box.** A camera key appears next to the picture key when a camera is attached. On the desktop it opens a viewfinder, with a camera picker if you have more than one. The photo is shrunk to 2048 pixels and stripped of location and camera details before it joins your message.
- **Make something** on Home offers ready-made craftbooks, such as a research report, a Word document, a slide deck, a PDF report, a website or an animated slideshow, with **See all** for the rest.
- **Knowledge catalogs open in Knowledge.** The **+** beside the catalog picker and **Browse knowledge catalogs** open a dialog in place, instead of sending you to Settings. Catalogs now have artwork.
- **OpenAI and Anthropic Claude with an API key** are always listed under **Settings → Artificial Intelligence**. Before, they were hidden until set up. Claude Sonnet 5 and 5.5 used with a key offer two more reasoning efforts, `xhigh` and `max`.
- **Model downloads keep going when you leave the page.** Speech, voice and image-recognition models now download in the background service. A second click joins the running download, and **Cancel** really stops it.
- **Local models are faster on a graphics card.** When a model fits entirely on the GPU, llama.cpp now uses at most four processor threads instead of all of them. On a DGX Spark, a small Gemma model went from 72 to about 120 tokens a second. A thread count you set yourself still wins.
- **Restarting gezel no longer crashes.** Restarting the service, for example from Settings, could end in a crash report. It now stops its model workers cleanly first.
- **Tasks no longer disappear.** A task gezel could not read back used to be created and then vanish. It is now refused when you create it, with the reason.
- **Errors from OpenAI now show**, instead of arriving as an empty answer. **Codex CLI** tries again when its connection to gezel's tools fails, instead of answering without them.
- **Mac `._` files are no longer indexed.** These hidden companion files failed to index and were tried again every night.
- Dependencies: `@bendyline/gilde` 0.1.93 and Squisq 2.11.17. The native engines are unchanged at 0.1.48.

## In the terminal

If you use the `gezel` command ([The gezel command line](../technical/cli-reference.md)):

- **`gezel <craftbook>` is short for `gezel do <craftbook>`.** Built-in commands always win, so a typo still says the command is unknown.
- **`gezel do` keeps your request.** Words without a `key=` used to fill the first parameter, usually the working folder. So `gezel do summarize-long "Summarize notes.txt"` named a folder after the sentence and lost the request. Words now fill only required settings with no default, and the rest becomes the request. `key=value` still sets anything.
- **`gezel do` checks setup before it starts.** If a craftbook needs External Services, web search or a model you haven't downloaded, a terminal asks yes or no for each, and a script stops before starting with the command that fixes it.
- **`gezel run -`** reads the prompt from standard input.
- **`gezel start --port` and `--web`** reuse a running service that already does that. `--restart` replaces one that doesn't.
- **Catalogs can include video and sound.** `gezel knowledge build` bundles MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC and similar files, and with the `embeddinggemma-2-512@1` profile and ffmpeg, they are searchable by what is in them. `catalog.json` can credit each file, and `--skip-images` now skips video and sound too. Catalogs with media use format 0.8, which older gezel releases cannot open. Other catalogs are still written as 0.7. See [Working with knowledge catalogs](../technical/knowledge-command-line.md).

## For developers

- **App SDK.** `streamText` takes `temperature`, `reasoningEffort` and `knowledge`, emits `progress` events, and reports `model` and `usage` on `done`. New: `withKnowledgeContext`, `connectDesktopEmbedding`, and a knowledge-only grant (`knowledge: true`). [DocBlocks: text AI with gezel](../technical/docblocks-text-ai-with-gezel.md) walks through a real integration.
- **OpenAI-compatible API.** `stream_options.include_progress` adds `gezel_progress` events. Bundled catalogs such as the Handboek are no longer offered to connected apps.
- **Scripts.** A `reminders` capability: `gezel.reminder.set({ at, title, body })` and `clear()`, at most 30 days ahead, one per project. See [Writing scripts](../technical/writing-scripts-with-gezel-sdk.md).
- **MCP tools.** New `copy_path`, `list_photos` and `photo_groups` (locations only for the person's app and on-device models). `save_memory` and `list_memories` take a `user` scope.
- **HTTP.** `/api/reminders`, `/api/media-search` (with install and ffmpeg re-check), project `albums`, `index/thumb`, `overview`, `file-summary` and `night-work` routes, `PUT /api/memory/lessons` (first-party only), and `DELETE` routes that cancel chat, speech and voice model downloads.
- **gezk 0.8** adds media assets and per-asset attribution, with JSON Schemas under `packages/gezk/schemas/0.8/`. The global CLI now needs `@huggingface/transformers` 4.3.1 or later. onnxruntime moves to 1.30.0.

## Things to see and do after update

Add a folder with **File → Add Folder…**, and check **This morning** on Home tomorrow. On the desktop, if you liked watching your gezels level up, turn on **Social mode** in **Settings → General**, and set how many **Notifications** a day you want just below it. To search video and sound as well as photos, install ffmpeg and look at **Settings → Image recognition**.

# What's new

Gezel changes often. This section is the running record of those changes, one article per release, newest first.

## Recent releases

::handboek-whats-new-list

Each article is written for the person using gezel, not for the person who wrote the code. It says what is different now, what you can do that you could not do before, and what — if anything — you need to do about it. Where a change is only interesting to developers, it is marked as such so you can skip it.

## How to read a version number

A gezel version looks like **1.26223.47**. The middle part is the date: `26` is the year 2026, and `223` is the day of that year — so `26223` is 11 August 2026. The last part is the build number for that day. You can always tell how old your copy of gezel is by reading the middle number.

Your installed version is shown in **Settings → About**.

## Updating

Packaged installs check for new releases on their own and offer the update when one is ready. Nothing in your work is touched by an update: your gezellen, projects, threads, memories and files all live in ordinary files on your disk, and a new version simply reads the same files.

Separately from app releases, gezel can pick up new catalog content — model descriptions, craftbooks, role templates — without a full update. That is the **Catalog content** setting under Settings → About, and it is off unless you turn it on.
